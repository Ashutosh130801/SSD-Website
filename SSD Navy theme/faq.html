Find clear answers regarding debt recovery operations, branch office coverage, RBI compliance standards, and portfolio handling by SSD Enterprises (Sri Sai Dhanada Enterprises).

SSD Enterprises handles a comprehensive range of retail and commercial credit products for Banks and NBFCs, including:

  • Credit Cards
  • Personal Loans (PL & PL Cross-Sell)
  • Business Loans (BL)
  • Consumer Durable Loans (CD)

SSD Enterprises operates an extensive regional network with 3 offices in Visakhapatnam (Vizag) including one head office and two other offices, then 4 more in Andhra Pradesh, 3 in Telangana, and 1 in Odisha. This multi-state footprint enables seamless field-collection and verification coverage for major financial institutions across territories.

SSD Enterprises maintains a zero-tolerance compliance policy across all branches. Every tele-calling station is connected to 100% recorded PRI interface lines, personal mobile phones are prohibited on calling floors, and staff undergo rigorous training on RBI guidelines regarding respectful customer communication and data confidentiality.

We operate across strategic branch locations covering Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, and Odisha, including Visakhapatnam (HQ & Madhavadhara branches), Vijayawada, Warangal, Karimnagar, Guntur, Secunderabad, Nellore, Kadapa, and Bhubaneswar.

SSD Enterprises provides full bucket coverage ranging from early-stage reminders (Bucket X, Buckets 2–3, Buckets 4–5) up to hardcore 180+ DPD write-off pools. Our team currently manages over ₹160 Cr+ in active portfolio mandates.

Financial institutions can contact our Head Office directly at Visakhapatnam. Contact S. Govinda Rao at +91 92908 65555 or email ssdenterprisesvizag@gmail.com to request agency credentials and discuss portfolio allocation.

We combine on-field physical address visits, tele-verification, and legal public-database lookups to trace non-contactable customers. Once located, a verified trace report is handed over to our dedicated recovery team for immediate follow-up.
