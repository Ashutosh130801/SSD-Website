ABOUT US & OUR LEGACY

Two Decades of Trusted Recovery

Quality Collections & Debt Recovery for Banks and NBFCs

Our journey began under the name SR Enterprises, which over the years grew to become a synonymous term across Andhra Pradesh for quality collection reporting for almost every leading NBFC and Bank. With experience dating back to 2006, we deployed a workforce of around 100 executives managing portfolios of up to ₹400 crores per month for the region's foremost financial institutions.

After a brief, deliberate pause, we returned with renewed vigour as SSD Enterprises — formally established in 2021. Under the banner of Sri Sai Dhanada Enterprises, we carry forward the same standards that earned our reputation, combining seasoned leadership with a modern, technology-enabled recovery operation. Our guiding philosophy remains unchanged: “Quality attracts Clients.”
